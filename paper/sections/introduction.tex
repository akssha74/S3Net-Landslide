\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

High-resolution optical imagery enables detailed landslide mapping, but roads, river deposits, clearings, and exposed rock share similar structure. HR-GLDD provides 3~m PlanetScope patches \citep{meena2023hrgldd}; CAS provides multisensor RGB imagery \citep{xu2024cas}; Landslide Reference Data (LRD) supplies named Sentinel-2 events \citep{orynbaikyzy2025lrd}.

NDVI is attractive because fresh failures can disrupt vegetation, but roads, agriculture, riverbanks, and shadows also create index edges. An index-modulated boundary loss changes both boundary emphasis and spectral modulation unless ordinary boundary weighting is controlled. Array semantics are equally load-bearing: assigning Red to the wrong column changes both NDVI and raw-edge controls.

This distinction matters for remote-sensing image processing. Devara et al. compared an empirical extraction method with U-Net for Sentinel-2 landslides \citep{devara2024landslide}, while Wang et al. designed edge enhancement for remote-sensing semantic segmentation \citep{wang2025saeenet}. These studies motivate boundary-specific outcomes and make generic edge and boundary controls necessary before a vegetation-specific interpretation can be claimed.

Known-order channel permutations test adaptability \citep{li2025bandadaptability}, not unresolved release metadata. We apply established identified-set logic to a full HR-GLDD physical-schema audit with controlled reruns; we do not claim a new sensitivity primitive.

We therefore evaluate components and semantic assumptions rather than presuming a mechanism. Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a semantic-identifiability certificate that reports whether signed contrasts retain direction over every metadata-consistent schema;
    \item an executable audit showing that HR-GLDD columns 0 and 2 remain ambiguously Red/Blue, followed by complete RGBN and BGRN sensitivity runs;
    \item capacity- and weight-matched zero, raw-edge, candidate-index, plain-boundary, and index-modulated controls;
    \item outcomes selected without test-label threshold tuning, plus post-hoc CAS and internally precommitted LRD stress analyses.
\end{enumerate}

The certificate identifies negative index-versus-generic F1 directions and a positive generic boundary direction, but leaves the index-modulated boundary direction unidentified. External analyses do not confirm transfer, and LRD defects make its result indeterminate rather than a robust rejection.

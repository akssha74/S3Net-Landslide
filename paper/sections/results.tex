Table~\ref{tab:controlled} reports every configuration under both candidate
orders; Supplementary Table S4 gives detailed RGBN-candidate operating metrics.
With base loss, candidate-index attention reaches $69.04\%$ F1 under
RGBN and $68.92\%$ under BGRN. Raw candidate-Red/NIR edges reach $69.05\%$ and
$69.13\%$, while the capacity-matched zero control reaches $68.88\%$ under
both. Candidate-index input specificity is therefore unsupported regardless
of order.
All contrasts and envelopes below use unrounded run-level values; subtracting
the table's two-decimal cells can differ by $0.01$ point.

Plain boundary weighting raises F1 in every seed and control mode under both
orders. Mean F1 spans $69.88$--$70.19\%$ and boundary F1
$55.85$--$56.20\%$. Equal-mass index modulation changes F1 by $-0.41$ points
under RGBN and $-0.33$ under BGRN. Its boundary-F1 contrast changes from
$+0.38$ to $-0.05$ points, so spectral modulation is not order-robust. The
order-stable result is generic boundary emphasis, not spectral identity.

\subsection{Semantic-identifiability certificate}

Here, ``certificate'' means the range of a contrast over both mappings; it
records whether the sign survives this finite sensitivity, not statistical
uncertainty.
The index-input-versus-raw F1 envelope is $[-0.21,-0.01]$ points and the
index-versus-plain-loss F1 envelope $[-0.41,-0.33]$, identifying negative
directions over the two declared candidates without assigning equal
plausibility. The corresponding boundary-F1
envelope $[-0.05,+0.38]$ crosses zero and is direction-unidentified. Generic
plain-boundary F1 has envelope $[+0.96,+1.13]$; all 18 individual effects lie
between $+0.42$ and $+1.62$ points. Supplementary Table~S5 is the generated
certificate.

\subsection{External analyses}

The post-hoc CAS sensitivity fails three of four criteria: plain boundary
weighting changes mean region-macro F1 by $-2.19$ points and boundary F1 by
$-1.86$ points, with mixed seed effects. It receives no confirmation credit
(Supplementary Table~S1).

The internally precommitted LRD run fails all four criteria but is not
confirmation-grade. Validation F1 is $0.008$--$0.151$; selected-threshold mean
F1 changes by $-4.73$ points. Its frozen boundary effect is $-24.10$ points,
but alternatives span $-0.15$ to $-4.92$, and two protected EIDs share a
trigger family with development. It is failed/indeterminate, not a robust
rejection (Supplementary Method~S2).

The publicly locked Sen12 test is verified-not-confirmed. Its sole passing
condition is the NDVI-attention-minus-raw-edge F1 envelope: $+0.70$ points
($[+0.28,+1.16]$). Generic boundary F1 is $+0.61$
($[-0.09,+1.32]$), with 5/10 inventories positive. All arm means are only
$1.21$--$3.14\%$ F1. The registered transformed-envelope check also fails for
one Kyrgyzstan pair, although native rectangles only share an edge. This
provides no support for recurrence and no refutation (Supplementary Method and
Table~S3).

\subsection{Operating point and spatial degradation}

At threshold $0.5$, boundary-weighted residual-attention FPR is
$2.47$--$2.50\%$. Validation-matched recall is exact in 53/54 runs; combined
matched FPR/precision spans $1.97$--$2.01\%$/$78.41$--$78.74\%$. This does not
support index-specific false-positive suppression.

Controlled $128\rightarrow38\rightarrow128$ degradation lowers F1 by
$4.33$--$5.20$ points across orders, identifying same-sensor
spatial-information sensitivity rather than cross-sensor causation.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

HR-GLDD does not authoritatively bind Red and Blue to columns 0 and 2. The certificate is finite over these two candidate schemas and descriptive means; it neither identifies wavelengths nor supplies statistical uncertainty. The arrays also omit event, scene, and coordinate mappings, precluding event-clustered or spatial inference; prior test inspection makes the evaluation corrective.

Binary masks omit background categories, so Supplementary Figure~S1 supports visual inspection but not category-specific error rates. The 10-m control models PlanetScope smoothing and resampling, not Sentinel-2 radiometry or geography.

The post-hoc CAS analysis jointly changes sensor, resolution, geography, and annotation. LRD has no independently timestamped pre-access record; two protected EIDs share a trigger family with development; 84.1\% of protected crops are empty; and validation F1 is weak. Its frozen criteria fail, but the external conclusion is indeterminate.

Three seeds quantify optimisation variation, not independent events. No regional mosaic, acquisition pipeline, geospatial reconstruction, hardware payload, or responder workflow was evaluated; operational deployment and broad geographic generalization remain outside scope.

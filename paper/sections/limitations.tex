\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

HR-GLDD does not authoritatively bind Red and Blue to columns 0 and 2. The certificate is finite over these two candidate schemas and descriptive means; it neither identifies wavelengths nor supplies statistical uncertainty. The arrays also omit event, scene, and coordinate mappings, precluding event-clustered or spatial inference; prior test inspection makes the evaluation corrective.

Binary masks omit background categories, so Supplementary Figure~S1 supports visual inspection but not category-specific error rates. The 10-m control measures sensitivity to PlanetScope smoothing and resampling, not Sentinel-2 radiometry or geography.

The post-hoc CAS analysis jointly changes sensor, resolution, geography, and annotation. LRD has no independently timestamped pre-access record; two protected EIDs share a trigger family with development; 84.1\% of protected crops are empty; and validation F1 is weak. Its frozen criteria fail, but the external conclusion is indeterminate.

Sen12 used development-driven calibration without confirmation credit; v6 corrected one descriptive SCL field after access but before inference. Arm F1 is below 3.2\%. Its registered transformed-envelope bar fails for abutting Kyrgyzstan tiles despite zero native positive-area overlap, precluding clean confirmation or refutation.

Three seeds quantify optimization variation, not independent events. No regional mosaic, acquisition pipeline, geospatial reconstruction, hardware payload, or responder workflow was evaluated; operational deployment and broad geographic generalization remain outside scope.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
Three limitations should be noted:
(1)~\textit{Optical Cloud Occlusion:} Persistent cloud cover obstructs optical imaging; coupling S$^3$-Net with radar backscatter from Sentinel-1 or NISAR remains future work.
(2)~\textit{Extreme Arid Terrain:} In hyper-arid zones devoid of canopy, background NDVI is low ($\text{NDVI} < 0.15$), diminishing scarp contrast.
(3)~\textit{Elevation Metadata:} Omitting DEMs ensures rapid deployment, but incorporating slope could refine deposit runout boundaries.

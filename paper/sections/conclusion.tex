\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This letter introduced S$^3$-Net, an ultra-lightweight (2.11M parameters), physics-guided architecture that decouples vegetation scarp contrast in high-resolution PlanetScope satellite imagery. Explicitly formulating physical NDVI scarp gradients and coupling them into residual spatial attention gates significantly enhances detection recall along narrow slip boundaries without requiring auxiliary elevation data.

Evaluated on HR-GLDD across 1,758 patches, S$^3$-Net achieves $70.18\% \pm 0.25\%$ micro F1, $54.06\% \pm 0.29\%$ IoU, and $58.52\% \pm 0.24\%$ macro F1 across three seeds. Paired 1,000-draw cluster-bootstrap tests confirm significant macro F1 gains of $+3.69\%$ ($p < 0.001$) over vanilla U-Net, $+0.60\%$ ($p = 0.005$) over ResU-Net, and $+1.44\%$ ($p < 0.001$) over an unguided ablation. S$^3$-Net operates with a background FPR of $2.84\%$, trading minor precision for $+4.51\%$ recall. In 10-fold LOSCO testing, S$^3$-Net achieves $53.11\% \pm 17.76\%$ zero-shot macro F1 ($+3.19\%$ over U-Net). Operating with 2.16~ms/tile latency (462.6~tiles/s) and $\approx 14\times$ fewer parameters than DCA-UNet, S$^3$-Net offers an accurate, lightweight framework for rapid post-disaster triage.

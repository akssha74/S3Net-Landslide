\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Dataset and scope of inference}

HR-GLDD provides 1,119/284/355 train/validation/test patches
\citep{meena2023hrgldd}; positive-pixel prevalence is
10.09/10.17/10.92\%. It supplies no row-level event, scene, or coordinates, so
patch independence is unverified. The test set was previously inspected.
Results are therefore corrective and descriptive across seeds, without
tile-bootstrap inference or geographic-generalization claims.

\subsection{Factorial controls}

Zero, raw-edge, and candidate-index controls cross base and plain-boundary
losses; one index arm uses equal-mass modulation. With U-Net and ResU-Net this
gives nine configurations, each trained with seeds 42--44 under both mappings
(54 runs). All use AdamW \citep{loshchilov2019adamw}, learning rate
$1.5\times10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, cosine decay, batch size 32, and
15 epochs; validation F1 selects the epoch.

\subsection{External analyses}

CAS compares base and plain-boundary loss post hoc without confirmation credit
\citep{xu2024cas}. LRD uses named Sentinel-2 bands and 28 EIDs, but lacks a
third-party pre-access timestamp and has trigger-family overlap
\citep{orynbaikyzy2025lrd}. Sen12Landslides freezes 21 checkpoints before
authorized evaluation of 1,889 patches from ten inventories
\citep{hohn2025sen12}; its five conditions cover two margins, generic-boundary
recurrence, absolute performance, and spatial integrity. A disclosed SCL-255
correction changed no input, mask, endpoint, or condition. Supplementary
Methods~S1--S3 give all protocols and deviations.

\subsection{Interpretation rules}

Input specificity requires improvement over zero and raw controls under both
orders. Loss specificity requires index modulation to beat equal-mass plain
weighting with stable direction. Complete-system gains override neither
control; LRD is ineligible for confirmation, and Sen12 requires all five
registered conditions.

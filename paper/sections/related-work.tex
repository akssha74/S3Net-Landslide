\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Landslide segmentation}

U-Net and residual-block U-Net architectures provide standard segmentation baselines \citep{ronneberger2015unet,zhang2018resunet}. HR-GLDD is a PlanetScope benchmark \citep{meena2023hrgldd}, CAS provides multisensor RGB patches \citep{xu2024cas}, and LRD and Sen12Landslides provide named Sentinel-2 data \citep{orynbaikyzy2025lrd,hohn2025sen12}. In Remote Sensing Letters, Devara et al. compare U-Net with an empirical Sentinel-2 rule \citep{devara2024landslide}. These performance studies do not isolate the active component.

\subsection{Spectral and edge guidance}

NDVI is a Red/NIR ratio, so a shared multiplicative term cancels algebraically;
real mountain illumination still produces documented topographic sensitivity
\citep{matsushita2007sensitivity}. TriGEFNet reports an NDVI-guided benefit
\citep{trigefnet2026}; spectral indices have also been combined with edge
extraction \citep{zhang2023boosting}, and SAEENet adds generic boundary guidance
\citep{wang2025saeenet}. Li et al. permute known HLS bands to test adaptability
\citep{li2025bandadaptability}; we instead audit unresolved released-array
provenance with zero, raw, and equal-mass controls.

\subsection{Finite sensitivity and identified sets}

Functional ANOVA has shown that architecture, fine-tuning, learning strategy,
and initialization contribute differently across remote-sensing classification
datasets \citep{shiri2026fanova}. That work decomposes variability across known
configurations; here the uncertain input is physical channel identity.
Multiverse analysis propagates reasonable coding choices
\citep{steegen2016multiverse}, while partial identification retains sets under
structural uncertainty \citep{manski2021partial}. Qin and Tong make
family-relative identified sets executable for evaluation claims
\citep{qin2026evaluation}. Our min--max rule applies that established primitive
to physical-schema uncertainty, with full retraining, same-class controls, and
a hash-bound claim record.

\subsection{Boundary weighting and evaluation}

Pixel-wise boundary emphasis predates this application in U-Net \citep{ronneberger2015unet}. LSL-SS-Net uses a landslide-specific level-set loss, while E-MSLDNet reports edge-aware CAS segmentation with boundary metrics \citep{yang2024lslssnet,zhao2025emsldnet}. We do not propose another boundary architecture or loss; we test whether index-specific attribution survives plain-boundary same-class control and unresolved physical schema.

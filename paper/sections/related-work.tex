\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Landslide segmentation}

U-Net and residual-block U-Net architectures provide standard segmentation baselines \citep{ronneberger2015unet,zhang2018resunet}. HR-GLDD is a PlanetScope benchmark \citep{meena2023hrgldd}, while CAS provides multisensor RGB patches \citep{xu2024cas}. In Remote Sensing Letters, Devara et al. compare U-Net with an empirical Sentinel-2 rule \citep{devara2024landslide}. These performance studies do not isolate the active component.

\subsection{Spectral and edge guidance}

NDVI is defined from Red and NIR reflectance and can reduce a shared multiplicative illumination factor under an idealized model \citep{matsushita2007sensitivity}. Soares et al. report NDVI-assisted cross-location balance \citep{soares2022generalization}, while TriGEFNet reports an NDVI-guided benefit \citep{trigefnet2026}. Spectral indices have also been combined directly with edge extraction \citep{zhang2023boosting}, while feature selection can exclude engineered features \citep{ahmad2026sequential}. SAEENet and weak-boundary extraction add generic boundary guidance \citep{wang2025saeenet,wang2026water}. Li et al. deliberately permute six known HLS bands to test foundation-model adaptability \citep{li2025bandadaptability}; we instead retain unresolved released-array identity and test attribution with zero, raw, and equal-mass controls.

\subsection{Finite sensitivity and identified sets}

Multiverse analysis propagates reasonable coding choices \citep{steegen2016multiverse}, while partial identification retains sets under structural uncertainty \citep{manski2021partial}. Qin and Tong make family-relative identified sets executable for evaluation claims \citep{qin2026evaluation}. Our min--max sign rule applies that established primitive; the remote-sensing delta is the physical-schema construction, full retraining, same-class controls, and hash-bound HR-GLDD claim record.

\subsection{Boundary weighting and evaluation}

Pixel-wise boundary emphasis predates this application in U-Net \citep{ronneberger2015unet}. We do not claim the first boundary-aware landslide method. Our domain contribution reports which attribution directions survive every metadata-consistent schema, using dual-order and equal-mass same-class controls.
